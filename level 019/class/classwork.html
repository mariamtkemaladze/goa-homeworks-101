Subaru Forester 2026 hybrid, made in indiana



The 2026 Subaru Forester Hybrid pairs a 2.5-liter 4-cylinder gas engine with dual electric motors for an intelligent,
continuous all-wheel-drive system. It produces a combined 194 horsepower, delivers an EPA-estimated 35 mpg combined,
and features a 1,500-lb towing capacity. Pricing ranges from approximately $34,730 to $41,545 depending on the trim.






Porsche 911, produced by Porsche AG



The Porsche 911 offers iconic sports car performance with a rear-engine layout,
available across multiple trims like the base Carrera, hybrid GTS, track-ready GT3, and Turbo. Depending on the model,
it delivers between 388 hp and 650 hp, achieving 0-60 mph times as quick as 2.7 seconds.






Nissan Skyline R34, manufactured by the Nissan Motor Company



The Nissan Skyline R34 (1998–2002) is the iconic eleventh generation of Nissan's performance line,
best known for the legendary GT-R variant. Celebrated for its advanced all-wheel-drive system and highly tunable 2.6-liter twin-turbo engine,
it has become a globally revered Japanese Domestic Market (JDM) icon.
