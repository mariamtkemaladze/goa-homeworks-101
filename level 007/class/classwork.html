pizza recipe

Ingredients: Dough: 1 and 1/3 cups warm water (\(110^{\circ}F\)), 1 packet (2 & 1/4 tsp) instant yeast, 1 tbsp sugar, 2 tbsp olive oil, 3.5 to 4 cups all-purpose flour, 1 tsp salt.Toppings: 1 cup pizza sauce, 4 cups shredded mozzarella, pepperoni, etc..

Instructions: Activate Yeast: Whisk warm water, sugar, and yeast in a bowl; let rest for 5 minutes.Make Dough: Add flour, olive oil, and salt. Mix until the dough pulls away from the sides of the bowl. Knead for 2 minutes.Rise: Cover dough and let it rise in a warm spot for 60–90 minutes until doubled in size.Shape & Top: Preheat oven to \(475^{\circ}F\) (\(245^{\circ}C\)). Divide dough in half. Roll or stretch into two 12-inch circles on a baking sheet sprinkled with cornmeal. Top with sauce, cheese, and toppings.Bake: Bake for 10–15 minutes until the crust is golden and cheese is bubbly.
