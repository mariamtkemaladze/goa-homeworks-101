Pizza

Make a quick, delicious homemade pizza by combining warm water, yeast, flour, olive oil, and salt.
Knead the dough and let it rise for one hour. Roll it out, add pizza sauce,
mozzarella cheese, and your favorite toppings, then bake in a hot oven until the crust is golden.
